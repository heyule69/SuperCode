## 1 · 极简几何

原图：supercode-icon-ai-v2-minimal.png

```text

Inspired by the visual confidence and economy of Codex/OpenAI and Claude, while having its own unmistakable identity. Strong silhouette, excellent legibility at 32 pixels. Exactly one icon, centered, square canvas. Generous equal padding, rounded-square tile occupies 86% of the canvas. Real alpha transparency outside the tile. No words, no label, no watermark, no presentation board, no phone or desktop mockup, no animals, no plants, no extra floating symbols, no complex tiny details.
Direction: rigorous minimalist black-and-white geometry, flat vector-like.
A very simple, compact monogram made from two broad complementary geometric strokes that interlock into an abstract S/C signature. A continuous rounded angular contour, intentional negative spaces, balanced construction, a few decisive shapes. Ivory-white mark on an almost-black charcoal tile. Both strokes feel equally integrated; the C is suggested by open negative space rather than literal typed letters. Extremely clean, bold and iconic, with no decorative spark or rays. Flat solid colors, no gradients, no shadows, no bevels or texture. Do not recreate the OpenAI knot.
```
