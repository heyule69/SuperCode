## 最终提示词

+Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one finished desktop application icon for SuperCode, an elegant multi-agent coding workspace.
Primary request: Create an original SuperCode identity inspired by the visual restraint, geometric continuity and interlocking construction of the Codex/OpenAI emblem, and the warm orange, confident radial energy of Claude's emblem.
Design: a single bold custom S-shaped emblem made from two interlocking rounded angular ribbon strokes, with a few broad, carefully spaced terminals suggesting a compact spark. The S must remain unmistakable at small size. Use generous negative space and uniform bold stroke weights. Integrate the spark quality into the S itself, not as an additional floating symbol.
Color palette: ivory white main stroke with one restrained warm terracotta-orange section; charcoal near-black rounded-square app tile. Matte solid colors.
Composition: exactly one front-facing square app icon, centered on a square 1024 by 1024 canvas. Rounded-square tile occupies about 86% of the canvas with equal transparent margins. Emblem occupies about 65% of the tile. Actual transparency outside the rounded-square tile, clean alpha edges.
Style: clean flat vector-like graphic, precise curves, strongly recognizable silhouette, balanced professional software brand. Only the tile and the emblem.
Constraints: no words, no SuperCode wordmark, no typography besides the custom S-shaped mark, no watermark, no mockup, no multiple options, no border, no artificial checkerboard, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no 3D, no tiny circuit details. Original geometry, do not recreate the existing OpenAI knot or Claude asterisk.
